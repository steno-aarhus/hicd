Available data

The data available in HICD

Questionnaire data

The following information was collected through questionnaire in 2020:

  • Anthropometrics (BMI, blood pressure)
  • Dental health
  • Diabetes distress (PAID)
  • Diabetes-specific problems and motivation
  • Diabetic status
  • Dyspnoea
  • Fertility (miscarriagesm abortions, contraceptives)
  • Health behavior (Smoking, alcohol, leisure time activity level)
  • Mental well-being (WHO5)
  • Patient Activation Measure (PAM)
  • Social network
  • Sleeping problems
  • Symptoms and diseases (Stomach, respiratory, pain, sexual dysfunktion, comorbidities)
  • Taste and smell

Register data

The following register data are available in the HICD:

  • Danish National Patient Register
  • Danish National Health Service Registry
  • Danish National Prescription Register Statistic
  • Clinical Laboratory Information System (LABKA), The Central Denmark Region
  • Register of Laboratory Results for Research
  • Cause of Death Register

Most of the registers data are available more than 20 years before inclusion in the cohort.
